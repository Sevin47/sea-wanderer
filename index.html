Sea Wanderer

The Wanderer sails into the mist on her own. Keep her crewed, fed and armed,
chart the sea, and find the Drowned Crown.
The Wanderer picks her own course. Everything she charts can be sold to a port's chart office. Her crew eat food every day, her carpenters patch the hull with wood, and her guns fire metal. Landing parties gather supplies on islands, pirates carry plunder, and merchants and port cities trade. Spend it all in Charts & Rigging, including room for more crew.
Black sails fight to the death. You choose to fight or flee; the rest is up to your preparation. If the Wanderer sinks, the voyage is over. Turn on Hands-off and she makes every decision herself: port calls, refits and battles.
